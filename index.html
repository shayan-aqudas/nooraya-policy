LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy for Nooraya

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Nooraya (Guided Soul) ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and providing a serene, distraction-free Islamic experience. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we process, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Information Collection and Use

Nooraya operates primarily on-device without requiring you to create an account, register, or provide personal identity credentials (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

  • Location Information: If granted, approximate/coarse location is accessed exclusively on your local device to calculate accurate Islamic prayer times and Qibla compass direction. Your location data is never sent to our servers, never stored remotely, and never shared or sold.
  • Audio & Playback Data: Playback state, last read Surah, bookmarks, and audio playback settings are stored locally on your device for your convenience.
  • Offline Downloads: Downloaded recitations and audio clips are saved locally to your device storage and can be removed by you at any time.
  • Notifications: We use local notifications to deliver optional prayer alerts and Surah reminders based on your chosen schedule.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Nooraya Gold)

Nooraya offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) via Google Play Billing.

  • All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play. We do not collect, view, or store your credit card or financial information.
  • Subscription tokens and active status are linked to your Google Play account for purchase restoration.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support free access to Quranic tools, the free version of Nooraya displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Subscribing to Nooraya Gold removes all advertisements completely.

Third-party SDKs used in the app include:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays ads according to Google's Privacy Policy. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous diagnostics and crash data to maintain stability and performance.

5. Android Auto Integration

Nooraya provides in-car audio browsing and recitation streaming via Android Auto MediaBrowserService. No car telemetry, driving data, or vehicle identity is recorded or processed.

6. Children's Privacy

Nooraya is suitable for users of all ages, including families and children. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
